# Thinking Orb Mind Reader

Build a mysterious and accurate 20Q mind-reading game inspired by Radica's classic 20Q handheld game.

Design & Aesthetic:
- Centerpiece: A glowing retro-futuristic orb as the psychic entity with atmospheric animations (CRT bloom, scanlines, pulsing core, particle aura, and audio/synth hums).
- Visual theme: Moody 1980s experimental AI / retro-laboratory terminal with monochrome/amber/phosphor green or synthwave cyan neon accents.

Gameplay & Mechanics:
- The player thinks of anything (object, person, creature, place, or concept).
- The Orb deduces what the player is thinking by asking up to 20 perceptive, dynamic questions (with early guess attempts if confidence spikes).
- Primary controls: Tactile retro response buttons: [Yes], [No], [Sometimes], [Doubtful], [Irrelevant].
- Joker mechanic ('Ask the Orb'): Once per game, the player can invoke their Joker wildcard to turn the tables, typing a free-text question to probe the Orb's omniscience. The Orb responds with cryptic, eerie insight before resuming its deduction.
- Atmospheric feedback: Cryptic commentary between turns, animated psychic confidence gauge, and a dramatic final mind-reading revelation sequence.
